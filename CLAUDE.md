# BikeSim (bikesim.org), formerly RideSim DC (ridesimdc.com)

"Feel it before you ride it." National since 2026-10-05: 13 US cities at bikesim.org/<city> (ridesimdc.com redirects); the DC history below still applies to DC. Pain point: people who want to bike can't tell what a trip will feel like before they go; maps show a line, not that block 3 is a six-lane arterial. RideSim is one feature, a ride simulator: pick a trip, see the stress of every block (RideScore DC LTS), then ride it virtually through Google Street View photos (default) or in our 3D model. Built on RideScore DC data for the Civic Tech DC hackathon (Oct 3, 2026). UI/UX and build conventions follow William's Web App Building Standard: https://github.com/william-wei-zhu/web-app-building-standard

## Layout
- `lib/cities.ts` city registry (view, city box, search hint, stress credit, examples, transit name, aerial tiles, `data` build date). Every city-specific value lives here. `cityDataBase(city)` = `${NEXT_PUBLIC_DATA_BASE || "https://storage.googleapis.com/bikesim-data"}/<slug>/<data>`.
- `lib/auth.ts` Firebase Auth + Firestore (lazy-loaded): Google or emailed sign-in link, the Street View gate, saved trips. `components/AuthSheet.tsx`, `components/useAccount.ts`, `app/settings/AccountSection.tsx`. Rules in `firestore.rules`.
- `pipeline/` bikesim-data (Python + uv): OSM extract -> `network.bin`, `streets.pmtiles`, `blocks.json`, `pois.json`, `meta.json` per city. `scripts/build_all.py` (download, clip, build), `scripts/publish.sh <slug> <date>` (upload to the bucket + archive), `scripts/city_stats.py` (home page km), `bikesim-data convert` (DC's RideScore JSON -> bin + pmtiles), `python -m bikesim_data.calibrate` (OSM model vs RideScore DC).
- `app/` Next.js 16 App Router. `/` is the city picker (`components/CityPicker.tsx`); `/[city]` is the map app (client-only via `components/app/MapAppLoader.tsx`, static for live cities, 404 otherwise). `app/api/geocode?city=` proxies Nominatim (bounded to the city box, per-IP limit, 24h in-memory cache; no city means DC).
- `lib/engine/` framework-free TypeScript (no React imports):
  - `net.ts` loads `network.bin` (`parseNetBin`; layout documented in `pipeline/src/bikesim_data/binary.py`) or the legacy `network.json` into typed arrays + CSR adjacency + grid index; `COMMUTER_LTS = 3` is the only rider.
  - `graph.ts` Dijkstra routing (`routePair`: shortest + lowest-stress with a 25x penalty on LTS 4; leftover LTS 4 edges are the unavoidable hostile stretches) and `stretches()` (route split by stress + street).
  - `geom.ts` small GeoJSON helpers (the street layer is a PMTiles vector source now).
  - `map.ts` MapLibre layers: flat stress-colored streets (PMTiles `streets` layer, `lts` property, via the `pmtiles://` protocol), white 3D buildings, route with a stress `line-gradient`, Start/End pins.
  - `ride.ts` first-person fly-through; frames carry position + heading for the other views.
  - `streetview.ts` Google StreetViewPanorama that follows the rider (lazy).
- `app/about`, `app/privacy`, `app/settings` (theme, default ride view, rider male/female; saved via `lib/prefs.ts` in localStorage); content pages share `components/SiteFrame.tsx`.
- `components/app/`: `MapApp` (state, views), `TripPanel` (trip inputs, start button, route choice, stress bar, hostile stretches), `RideHud` (stress meter, view and speed switches).
- City data lives in the public GCS bucket `gs://bikesim-data/<slug>/<date>/` (project ridesimdc; CORS for bikesim.org, ridesimdc.com, ridescoredc.com, localhost; immutable cache, so a rebuild gets a new date and `data` in cities.ts is bumped). `public/data/dc/` keeps DC's legacy `network.json` for RideScore DC's "Ride it" page (old `/data/*.json` paths rewrite there).
- `gs://bikesim-archive` (private, versioned): source extracts (clipped), official layers, every build, the DC RideScore data, brand originals.
- Theme tokens derive from the logo: `../brand/THEME.md` is the source of truth; `app/globals.css` mirrors it.

## Scaling
Streets render from PMTiles, so the map scales to any city. Routing loads the whole city's `network.bin` into memory on the main thread: fine up to LA's 224k segments (6.3 MB gz, well under a second to parse on a laptop). Past ~500k segments, split the graph by tile or move routing to a Web Worker.
